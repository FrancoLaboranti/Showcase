Lanternvale

A small adventure in a valley that its lanterns keep safe. Take quests in the village, go out and fight, bring the loot home, sell it, buy better gear and push on into the next zone. The game saves by itself.
Desktop
  • WASD or the arrows move. Space or J attacks (hold it to keep swinging), K or Shift dodges.
  • 1234 skills, Q drinks a potion, E talks, opens and picks.
  • I bag, L quests, M map, P or Esc pauses. The mouse works every menu.
Mobile
  • The joystick is born wherever you touch the left half of the screen.
  • The big button attacks. Around it: dodge, four skills and a potion. A TALK / OPEN button pops up when something is within reach; tapping a person or a chest works too.
How it plays
  • Attacks aim at the nearest monster by themselves; three quick swings make a combo. Dodging makes you untouchable for a moment. Red marks on the ground are attacks about to land.
  • Skills unlock with levels (2, 4, 7 and 10) and recharge on their own. Levelling up also heals you completely.
  • Monsters drop gold, materials and sometimes gear: common, uncommon, rare, epic. Champions with a golden rim always drop gear.
  • Every merchant buys whatever you carry. Sister Wren heals you for free. If you fall you wake up in the village with a little less gold.
  • New zones open through the main quests (the orange diamond in the log). The arrow at the edge of the screen points at your current goal. Chests, hidden paths and rare monsters reward a look around.
  • Monsters come back after a while, bosses after five minutes.
The buttons
  • pauses, saves and goes back to the title, sound on/off.
  • fullscreen, leaves for the Arcade (your progress is kept).
