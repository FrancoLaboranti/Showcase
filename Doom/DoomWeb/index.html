Doom

You go down, floor by floor, through a foundry buried under the city, where the slag has come alive. Every sublevel is a short maze: find the coloured keys for its doors, reach the cargo lift and pull its lever to go deeper. Every floor hides secrets: panels that open when you use them, and cracked walls that fall to gunfire.
Desktop
  • Click the screen to capture the mouse; the mouse looks and the left button fires (F fires too).
  • W A S D move and strafe; the arrows move and turn; Shift runs.
  • E or Space opens doors and pulls levers; so does the right button.
  • 1 2 3 4, the wheel or Q change weapon. Tab or M show the map.
  • Esc or P pause.
Mobile
  • Left thumb: the stick appears where you touch; push it to the rim to run.
  • Right thumb: drag to look. FIRE shoots and, held down, aims as well.
  • The buttons beside it use (doors, levers), switch weapon and open the map.
  • Pause and Back to menu sit at the top right.
The arsenal
  • Riveter: precise, and it never runs dry. Blunderbuss: scrap metal at arm's length.
  • Stitcher: a steady burst that spreads if you keep the trigger down. Crucible: a ball of slag that bursts over an area (and burns you if you are too close).
