Tron V2

Tron in a large world with walls and a camera that follows you. Hit a trail, a wall or yourself and you are de-rezzed. Your engine rises in pitch as a wall gets closer ahead — if you hear it open up, you are about to clip something. The minimap shows where everyone is.
