Chess

Full chess, with castling, en passant and promotion. You pick your opponent and colour in the menu. While you are reviewing the history you cannot move — return to the present first.
Desktop
  • Drag a piece, or click the square you are on and then the destination. The legal squares light up when you pick the piece up.
  • ← → walk the history move by move; Home and End jump to the start and to the present, same as Starting position and Current position.
  • R resets the board, N goes back to the menu, P or Space pauses.
Mobile
  • Drag a piece, or tap origin and then destination.
  • Previous / Next walk the history; Starting position and Current position jump to either end.
