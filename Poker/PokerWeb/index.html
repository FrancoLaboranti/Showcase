Poker

Heads-up Texas Hold'em against the CPU. Two cards of yours, five community cards, best five wins. The banner at the top says which hand you currently hold, so you never have to work it out. If you run out of chips, DEAL gives you a fresh stack.
Desktop
  • DEAL deals and posts the blinds.
  • CHECK passes without betting (only when nobody has bet); CALL matches the bet.
  • RAISE bets more, and the amount is set with + and −.
  • ALL IN pushes your whole stack. FOLD gives up the hand and the pot.
Mobile
  • The same buttons, tapped. They grey out when the action is not available.
Community cards come out three (flop), one (turn) and one (river).
