Stick Fight

A brawling sandbox. Clear the stage and reach the EXIT on the right to move on.
Desktop
  • AD or ←→ move, S crouches, W or Space jumps.
  • J punches, K kicks, L blocks, Shift dashes, P pauses.
Mobile
  • The joystick on the left half does the moving: sideways to walk, up to jump and down to crouch (and to let go of a ledge).
  • Four round buttons on the right, in two columns: DASH BLOCK on top, PUNCH KICK underneath.
  • DASH goes the way the joystick is pointing, or the way you are facing if it is centred. Double-tapping the joystick sideways still dashes.
The bar
  • pauses · back to the menu · sound on/off.
  • fullscreen · leaves for the Arcade.
Tuning panel
  • Live sliders for movement, gravity, jumping, poses and combat. What you move, you feel on the next frame.
  • Desktop: T opens and closes it.
  • Mobile: on this menu screen tap the STICKFIGHT title three times.
