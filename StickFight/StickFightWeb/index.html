Stick Fight

A brawling sandbox. Clear the stage and reach the EXIT on the right to move on. Blocking is holding the direction AWAY from the enemy — down-and-back blocks sweeps.
Desktop
  • AD or ←→ move, S crouches, W or Space jumps.
  • J punches, K kicks, Shift dashes, P pauses.
Mobile
  • The joystick on the left half moves; pull down to crouch.
  • PUNCH KICK JUMP are the three round buttons on the right.
  • Double-tap the joystick sideways to dash.
The bar
  • pauses · back to the menu · sound on/off.
  • fullscreen · leaves for the Arcade.
Combos
  • Punch x3: jab, cross, uppercut — the last one launches.
  • Punch, punch, kick: spinning finisher.
  • Dash + punch: a running hook. Run + kick: flying kick.
Climbing and recovery
  • Jump at a platform edge to hang; push in or jump to pull up. Against a wall you slide and can jump off it.
  • While you are flying, press jump, punch or kick to recover in mid-air. Landing while holding a direction rolls instead of dropping you flat; on the ground any button gets you up sooner.
