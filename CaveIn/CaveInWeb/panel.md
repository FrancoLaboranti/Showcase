# Cave-In

Bomb your way down a mine that is coming apart. Clear out every creature, find the **lift** hidden under a crate and ride it down. Four strata of four depths, and a boss waiting at the bottom of each.

## Controls
- touch: Put your thumb anywhere on the left half: the stick is born there.
- touch: {btnBomb} drop a bomb
- touch: {btnDet} fire the detonator (it shows up while you carry one)
- keys: [W] [A] [S] [D] move, or the arrows
- keys: [Space] / [J] drop a bomb
- keys: [K] fire the detonator
- keys: [P] / [Esc] pause
- keys: [M] sound on and off
- keys: Hold a second direction and you turn into the next opening by yourself.

## Bombs
- A bomb bursts in a cross. The glow on the floor is exactly where it will reach, and it burns brighter as the fuse runs out.
- Stone stops a blast. A crate breaks, and stops it too.
- Only your own cell counts: a blast that does not reach it cannot hurt you.
- A blast that reaches another bomb sets it off. In a chain, every creature is worth double the one before, up to x16.

## The lift
- It opens once every creature is gone.
- Do not bomb it: it spits out more, and for a moment they cannot be hurt.
- When the clock runs out, the mine caves in from the edges.
