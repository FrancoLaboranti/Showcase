Mini Marbles

Marbles again, but a lot smaller! The toy stops being some marbles and becomes a swarm. Several fingers throw several at once.
Desktop
  • Drag a marble and let go to throw it.
  • Q pins the one you are holding, same as Pin. Space makes them all jump, same as Jump.
  • Add a ball / Remove a ball, Wall mode (closed, no sides, no ceiling or floor, none at all) and Restart in the bar.
Mobile
  • Drag and release to throw. Several fingers at once throw several marbles.
  • The bar buttons behave the same as on desktop.
