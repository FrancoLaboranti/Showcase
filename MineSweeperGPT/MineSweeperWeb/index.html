Minesweeper

Clear the field without hitting a mine. A cell's number is how many mines are in the eight cells around it.
Desktop
  • Left click digs a cell. Right click plants or removes a flag.
  • R restarts, same as Restart (R).
  • Mode: Classic / Gold Rush and Difficulty: Easy / Medium / Hard in the bar.
Mobile
  • Tap a cell to dig it. Press and hold to plant or remove a flag.
Gold Rush adds buried gold, and it does not auto-clear — you have to dig it out. Difficulty changes the field size and how many mines it holds.
