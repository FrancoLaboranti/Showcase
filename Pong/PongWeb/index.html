Sleepy Pong

Pong, played in landscape. Two things are not standard: two points in a row set your paddle on fire, and the CPU falls asleep. When you see the Zzz, that is the window.
The menu
  • One Player and Two Players open a setup: the opponent, the points to win (5 to 40, or Practice for an endless match) and whether fire is on.
  • Challenge lines the five opponents up easiest first and you go through them in one run.
  • Bricks puts a wall behind each paddle: Defence plays for points, Time trial counts how much of it you break against a clock.
  • The five opponents differ in behaviour, not only in difficulty: one returns flat and never sleeps, another sprays the ball everywhere and dozes off constantly. Your longest rally and your best winning margin are kept for each one.
Desktop
  • ↑ ↓ move your paddle. In two-player, W S drive the left one.
  • P or Pause pauses; Back to menu leaves the match.
  • In the menu, ↑ ↓ pick and Enter confirms.
Mobile
  • Slide your finger up and down on your half of the screen.
  • Tap a menu option to choose it.
The rally climbs in pitch with every return: the higher it sounds, the longer the point you are in.
