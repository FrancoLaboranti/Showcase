Tic-Tac-Toe

Three in a row on a 3x3 board. The CPU searches the whole game tree, so it cannot be beaten: against it the best result is a draw. Who opens alternates every round.
Desktop
  • Click a cell to place your mark.
  • Change mode cycles 2 players, you as X vs CPU and you as O vs CPU.
  • New round clears the board; Reset the scoreboard wipes the X / O / draw tally.
Mobile
  • Tap a cell to place your mark.
  • The bar buttons behave the same as on desktop.
