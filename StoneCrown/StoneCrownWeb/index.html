Stone Crown

Real-time strategy. Gather resources, grow your village, train an army and raze the rival's base. The winner is whoever leaves the other without a Hearth and without villagers.
Desktop
  • Click selects a unit or a building; drag selects several. Shift+click adds or removes, a double click selects every unit of that type on screen.
  • Right-click gives the order that fits where it lands: move, attack, gather, build or repair. With a building selected, it sets the rally point.
  • The camera: WASD, the arrows or the mouse against the edge. The wheel zooms and the middle button drags the map.
  • E attack-move, X stop, Q idle villager, F the whole army, H the Hearth, Space centres the selection, Shift+1..9 saves a group and 1..9 recalls it (twice centres the camera), P pauses, Esc cancels.
Mobile
  • Tap a unit to select it; two taps select every unit of that type on screen. Hold your finger still for a moment and drag to select with a box.
  • With units selected, tap the ground to move, an enemy to attack or a resource to gather.
  • One finger drags the camera and two fingers pinch to zoom. The minimap moves the camera too.
  • To build: pick the building, tap where it goes (or drag its outline) and confirm with the tick.
Economy
  • Food from berries and farms, wood from trees, gold and stone from the mines. Villagers carry their load to the Hearth or to a Storehouse: the closer it is, the more it yields.
  • Each House adds 5 population. The Crown Age, researched at the Hearth, brings Knights, Towers and upgrades.
Combat
  • The Spearman stops the Knight, the Knight rides the Archer down and the Archer punishes the Spearman.
  • The Hearth and the Towers shoot at whatever comes close, on their own.
