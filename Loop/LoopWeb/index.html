Loop

You are a marble dragging a thread of light across a clock face.
Move the thread to deflect orbs and anything it bounces turns CHARGED to damage enemies. Swing the head and the orb flies where you are moving.
You can cross your own thread to close a LOOP and everything inside takes the hit. Tighter loop does more damage. Bigger loop for more points.
Claim sectors. Each of the nine sectors has a diamond at its centre. Loop around one to claim that sector. Three in a row triggers FRENZY once per hour, which clears the board, enemies are dragged toward you, you are untouchable, you kill on contact for double points and you heal back a third of your health bar.
Desktop
  • WASD or the arrow keys move.
  • Space or Shift is a dash: an invulnerable lunge that pulls the thread straight, pretty useful for closing loops.
  • E pulse: a shockwave that shoves everything away.
  • P pauses, H inspects your hand, T opens the tuning panel.
Mobile
  • Stick on the left half of the screen steers.
  • DASH and PULSE are the two buttons on the right.
The clock runs the show! One sweep of the hand is one HOUR: it chimes at 3, 6, 9 and 12, sending a shockwave from the centre. Every hour comes with a new RULE. The bolder the rule you take, the higher your STREAK multiplier. It only ever goes up, so the question is how much you can stomach, never what is safe.
