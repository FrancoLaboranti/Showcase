LOOP — you are a marble dragging a thread of light across a clock face.
The thread is everything. It deflects orbs like a Pong paddle. Anything it bounces turns CHARGED and damages enemies.
Swing the head of the thread and the orb flies where you are moving. Near the tail it just bounces.
Cross your own thread to close a LOOP. Everything inside takes the hit. Tighter loop = more damage. Bigger loop = more points.
Claim sectors: each of the nine sectors has a diamond at its center. Loop around a diamond to claim that sector. Three in a row = TIC-TAC-TOE, which triggers FRENZY: the board clears, enemies are dragged toward your hand, you are untouchable and you kill them on contact for double points. A shockwave fires when it ends.
Touch: stick on the left half · DASH (invulnerable lunge that pulls the thread straight — the tool for closing loops) · PULSE (shockwave that shoves everything away).
Keys: WASD / arrows · SPACE or SHIFT dash · E pulse · P pause · H inspect your hand · 1 2 3 pick · T tuning panel.
The clock runs the show. One sweep of the hand is one HOUR. It drops enemies from its tip and chimes at 12, 3, 6 and 9, sending a shockwave from the center.
Every hour you get one choice, alternating: a new RULE for the arena, or a new CARD for your build. Five cards make a poker hand — pairs, straights and flushes grant passives.
Survive 12 hours and bring down THE WATCHMAKER. Its armor shrugs off orbs: only loops closed around it do full damage.
