LOOP. You are a marble dragging a thread of light across a clock face.
The thread is everything. It deflects orbs like a Pong paddle. Anything it bounces turns CHARGED and damages enemies.
Swing the head of the thread and the orb flies where you are moving. Near the tail it just bounces. It does not stop enemy shells. Those you dodge.
Cross your own thread to close a LOOP. Everything inside takes the hit. Tighter loop = more damage. Bigger loop = more points.
Claim sectors: each of the nine sectors has a diamond at its center. Loop around a diamond to claim that sector. Three in a row = TIC-TAC-TOE, which triggers FRENZY once per hour: the board clears, enemies are dragged toward you, you are untouchable, you kill on contact for double points and you heal back a third of your bar. A shockwave fires when it ends.
Touch: stick on the left half. DASH is an invulnerable lunge that pulls the thread straight, and it is the tool for closing loops. PULSE is a shockwave that shoves everything away.
Keys: WASD / arrows, SPACE or SHIFT dash, E pulse, P pause, H inspect your hand, T tuning panel.
The clock runs the show. One sweep of the hand is one HOUR. It drops enemies from its tip and chimes at 12, 3, 6 and 9, sending a shockwave from the center.
Every hour ends with a RULE: three on offer, one for the hour to come. The bolder the rule you take, the higher your STREAK multiplier. It only ever goes up, so the question is how much you can stomach, never what is safe.
Cards are bought with score, not with hours: cross a threshold and the draft opens right there, mid-fight. Five cards make a poker hand: pairs, straights and flushes grant passives. Once the hand is full every new card is a replacement, and that is where the poker actually starts.
Survive 12 hours and bring down THE WATCHMAKER. Its armor halves everything except a loop closed around it. Watch the beat: it winds up, then its core opens. While open it cannot hurt you and it takes triple damage.
