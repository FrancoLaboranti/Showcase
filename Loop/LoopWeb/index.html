Loop

You are a marble dragging a thread of light across a clock face.
The thread is everything. It deflects orbs like a Pong paddle, and anything it bounces turns CHARGED and damages enemies. Swing the head and the orb flies where you are moving; near the tail it just bounces. It does not stop enemy shells — those you dodge.
Cross your own thread to close a LOOP: everything inside takes the hit. Tighter loop = more damage. Bigger loop = more points.
Claim sectors. Each of the nine sectors has a diamond at its centre; loop around one to claim that sector. Three in a row triggers FRENZY once per hour: the board clears, enemies are dragged toward you, you are untouchable, you kill on contact for double points and you heal back a third of your bar. A shockwave fires when it ends.
Desktop
  • W A S D or the arrow keys move.
  • Space or Shift dash — an invulnerable lunge that pulls the thread straight, and the tool for closing loops.
  • E pulse: a shockwave that shoves everything away.
  • P pauses, H inspects your hand, T opens the tuning panel.
Mobile
  • Stick on the left half of the screen steers.
  • DASH and PULSE are the two rings on the right.
The clock runs the show. One sweep of the hand is one HOUR: it drops enemies from its tip and chimes at 12, 3, 6 and 9, sending a shockwave from the centre. Every hour ends with a RULE — three on offer, one for the hour to come. The bolder the rule you take, the higher your STREAK multiplier. It only ever goes up, so the question is how much you can stomach, never what is safe.
