Tank WARS

Survive waves of enemy tanks; between waves you spend what you earned in the shop. Played in landscape.
Desktop
  • W A S D or the arrow keys drive. Move the mouse to aim and hold the left button to fire.
  • Right mouse button fires a homing missile, same as Missile.
  • Shift is a short boost, same as Boost. Q triggers rage.
  • Tab opens Shop (upgrades). P pauses.
Mobile
  • Left stick moves. Right stick aims and fires at the same time — you do not hold a separate trigger.
  • Missile and Boost are the buttons on the right.
  • Tap the rage bar at the top to trigger rage — there is no separate button for it.
On the field
  • Crates break open in one hit. Lootboxes take several and drop power-ups, cash or a shower of coins — the ping rises as one gets closer to breaking.
  • Mines hit hard. So does your own missile if you are standing next to it.
  • The shield absorbs shots until it breaks, and breaking is the only dissonant sound in the game.
