Tank WARS

Survive the onslaught destroying enemy tanks, and turn every victory into a deadlier machine!
Desktop
  • WASD or the arrow keys drive. Move the mouse to aim and hold the left button to fire.
  • Right mouse button fires a homing missile, same as Missile.
  • Shift to use boost, same as Boost. Q triggers rage.
  • Tab opens Shop (upgrades). P pauses.
Mobile
  • Left stick moves. Right stick aims and fires at the same time, so you do not hold a separate trigger.
  • Missile and Boost are the buttons on the right.
  • Tap the rage bar at the top to trigger it when it's full.
On the field
  • Crates block the way and you can break them to get more coins.
  • Lootboxes can give you coins, restore health or give you special upgrades!
  • Be careful of Mines nearby.
  • The shield blocks enemy fire and missiles. Once it breaks, you're wide open. Enemy missiles hit hard, so take cover until it recharges!
The menu
  • Unlock more colors for your tank! Wave 20, five bosses in one run, beating the Final Boss, 50,000 points and Endless x5.
Tank
The same power budget spread differently.
  • Standard: the baseline, with no trade-off in either direction.
  • Sniper: each shot hits 2.2x harder and the cannon takes 2.2x longer to fire again. The same damage per second, delivered in fewer and heavier hits, so a miss costs you. Missiles reload 20% slower.
  • Gunner: the mirror image. Shots do 45% less and the cannon fires 1.8x faster, so you correct your aim during the burst instead of committing to it. Missiles reload 20% sooner.
  • Bunker: takes 60% less damage and moves 40% slower.
  • Commando: +2 missiles in reserve and they reload 1.8x faster, with a cannon 15% weaker.
Mutators
They stack, they change the whole run, and each one pays you for what it takes away.
  • Glass Cannon: you deal 60% more damage and take 60% more. Every fight ends sooner, in both directions.
  • Swarm: +120% enemies, each with 45% less health and 20% more speed. Far fewer elites, +50% gold, +15% score.
  • No Power-ups: the purple lootbox stops offering the 1 of 3 upgrade draft and drops gold instead. +30% score and +25% gold.
  • Frenzy: you and every enemy move 30% faster. +15% score.
Difficulty
  • Both run the same 50 waves and the same Final Boss. Hardcore brings the next wave 35% sooner, gives the regular enemies 50% more health and lets their damage climb to 1.7x instead of 1.35x, makes 30% of them elites instead of 12%, keeps up to 14 mines on the floor instead of 9 and spaces the lootboxes about 1.5x further apart.
  • Each mode keeps its own best score, and the line under the button lists them side by side.
  • Killing the Final Boss on wave 50 opens Endless: the run goes on with bigger and bigger groups until you die, and it scores on a board of its own.
