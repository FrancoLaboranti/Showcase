Rumble Cup

Five-a-side arcade football where a tackle can be a punch, the pitch fights back and every player has a shot of their own. Score more than the other lot before the whistle.
Desktop
  • WASD or the arrows move, Shift sprints (it spends stamina, and a sprinting player pushes the ball further ahead).
  • J pass (hold for a lob) · K shoot (hold to charge) · L tackle · I special · Space switch player · P pause.
  • Without the ball K jumps, and a jump into a high ball is a header; J asks the teammate on the ball for a pass. Z X C V work as pass, shoot, tackle, special.
Mobile
  • The joystick on the left half moves; pushed all the way to the rim it sprints.
  • PASS SHOOT TACKLE on the bottom row, SWITCH SPECIAL above. The labels change with the situation: without the ball SHOOT reads JUMP, PASS reads CALL, and with it TACKLE reads BARGE.
The rough stuff
  • TACKLE next to a rival throws a punch, and three in a row flatten him. From further away it is a sliding tackle that takes the legs of whoever is on the ball.
  • With the ball, TACKLE is a shoulder barge: it clears a defender without losing it.
  • A downed player is out for a moment and cannot be hit again. Heavy players shrug off what sends a light one flying; a hard shot knocks over whoever it hits.
Specials
  • The bar under your score fills with tackles, knockdowns, passes and shots. With a segment full, SPECIAL fires the shot of whoever has the ball (or a high ball within reach). Every kind of player has a different one.
Pitches
  • Green Park: plain grass, the honest one.
  • Mudhole: rain turns the pitch to mud. The ball dies in it, feet slip, and sliding tackles and falls pile the mud into mounds you can stand on to reach a high ball.
  • Glacier Rink: ice. Nobody stops where they want to, the ball slides for ever and bounces off the boards, because there is no out. Snow drifts pile up against the boards.
  • Clifftop: a gale that turns. It bends every ball in the air, pushes light players around and blows crates across the pitch.
  • Crater 9: the moon. Low gravity, huge jumps, a floating ball and craters that swallow it.
Piling on
  • Jump onto a teammate to ride his shoulders and head balls nobody else can reach; land on a rival to flatten him. A heavy rider squashes a light one.
