Clock

An analogue clock in phosphor on black glass.
Desktop
  • Click the clock to switch the second hand between tick and sweep.
  • Second hand: tick / sweep is the same switch from the bar.
  • Clock colour cycles the seven palettes; Auto colour changes them on its own.
Mobile
  • Tap the clock to switch the second hand between tick and sweep.
  • The bar buttons do the same as on desktop.
