Clock

An analogue clock in phosphor on black glass. It shows the device's time — there is nothing to win.
Desktop
  • Click the clock — switches the second hand between tick (one jump per second) and sweep (continuous).
  • Second hand: tick / sweep — the same switch from the bar.
  • Clock colour cycles the seven palettes; Auto colour changes them every two seconds on its own.
Mobile
  • Tap the clock — switches the second hand between tick and sweep.
  • The bar buttons do the same as on desktop.
