Simon Says

The memory toy. The disc plays a sequence and you repeat it; each round adds one more at the end. One mistake ends the run. Each colour has a fixed note and the four form a chord, so the sequence can be remembered by ear as well as by sight — that is how the real toy worked.
Desktop
  • Click the centre to start.
  • Click the coloured quarters in the order they lit up.
  • Back to menu in the bar.
Mobile
  • Tap the centre to start, then tap the quarters in order.
