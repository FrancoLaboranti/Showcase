# Afterfall
> The town is dead. Noise is what gets you killed.

Every trip out of the shelter is a gamble: search the town for food, water, medicine and parts, and make it back before they close in. Fix the truck at the far end of town and you can leave for good. You can fight back, but killing never pays, and every shot is heard.

## The way out
- The truck needs a **car battery**, a **fuel can**, a **starter motor** and **10 scrap**. Fit them in any order; the game saves as you go.
- With every part fitted, start the engine and hold on by the truck until it is ready. The whole town hears it, and the shelter is closed to you.

## Controls
- touch: Left thumb walks (a light push sneaks). Right thumb turns and looks up and down.
- touch: {btnFire} attack (drag it to aim too)
- touch: {btnUse} use or pick up
- touch: {btnWeap} change weapon
- touch: {btnReload} reload
- touch: {btnBag} backpack
- keys: [W] [A] [S] [D] walk (or the arrows)
- keys: Mouse turns and looks up and down, click attacks.
- keys: [Shift] sneak (hold)
- keys: [E] use or pick up
- keys: [R] reload
- keys: [1] [2] [3] hatchet, pistol, shotgun (or the wheel)
- keys: [I] / [Tab] backpack
- keys: [H] [F] [G] medicine, food, water
- keys: [P] / [Esc] pause

## Staying alive
- **Noise draws them.** The hatchet is as loud as a footstep, the pistol carries far, the shotgun farther, and a scream farthest of all. Sneaking is quiet.
- Aim for the head: it takes three times the damage, and the hit marker turns gold.
- Hunger and thirst only drain outside. In the shelter time stands still, and a badly hurt survivor is patched up to 30 health.
- The backpack has 8 slots, so choose what comes home.
- **Death ends the run** and takes everything with it: the backpack, the stash and the truck's parts.
