# Afterfall

Scavenge a dead town, get back to the shelter alive, and repair the truck that can take you out. Killing is the last choice: every shot is heard. This whole file is a PROPOSAL that Franco edits before it is published.

## Controls
- touch: Left thumb walks (a light push sneaks). Right thumb turns.
- touch: {btnFire} attack  {btnUse} use  {btnWeap} weapon  {btnBag} backpack
- keys: [W] [A] [S] [D] walk
- keys: Mouse turns, click attacks.
- keys: [E] use or pick up
- keys: [1] [2] [3] hatchet, pistol, shotgun
- keys: [Shift] sneak (hold)
- keys: [I] backpack
- keys: [H] [F] [G] medicine, food, water
- touch: {btnPause} pause
- keys: [P] / [Esc] pause
- keys: [M] sound on and off

## How it works
- Noise brings them. The hatchet is almost silent, the pistol is heard far away, the shotgun farther, a scream farthest. Sneaking is quiet.
- The backpack holds 8 slots, and what you carry out can be lost. The shelter's stash is safe.
- Hunger and thirst run only outside. In the shelter time stands still and a battered survivor is patched up to 30 health.
- The truck needs a battery, fuel, a starter motor and scrap. Fit them in any order; the game saves as you go.
