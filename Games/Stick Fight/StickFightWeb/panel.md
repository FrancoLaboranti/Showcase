# Stick Fight
> Climb the platforms, drop every fighter, and the door opens.

You are an ink stickman on a paper world, brawling across floating platforms. Every level is a new climb with four to nine fighters, each with a style of their own, and the **EXIT** stays locked until all of them are down. Your punch and kick change with what you are doing, so a sweep, a flying kick or a lunge out of a dash is always one button away.

## Controls
- touch: Your left thumb is the stick, wherever it lands on the left half (it is not drawn). Sideways walks, **up jumps**, **down crouches**, and a quick double flick sideways dashes.
- touch: [PUNCH] [KICK] punch, kick
- touch: [BLOCK] block (holding the stick away from an attacker blocks too)
- touch: [DASH] dash where the stick points, or the way you face
- keys: [A] [D] move, or the arrows
- keys: [W] / [Space] jump, or [↑]
- keys: [S] crouch, or [↓]
- keys: [J] punch
- keys: [K] kick
- keys: [L] block (holding away from the attacker blocks too)
- keys: [Shift] dash the way you hold, or double-tap [A] / [D]
- keys: [P] pause
- keys: [T] the tuning panel, an extra toy: live sliders for movement, gravity, jumps, poses, combat and the camera.
- touch: Tap the title on the menu three times for the tuning panel, an extra toy: live sliders for movement, gravity, jumps, poses, combat and the camera.

## One button, many moves
- **Punch again** as a strike ends and it chains: jab, cross, hook, uppercut. **Kick** instead and the chain ends in a sweep, a roundhouse or a spinning kick.
- Crouched, the punch goes low and the kick is a sweep that knocks them down.
- Running, the kick is a flying dropkick. Out of a dash, the punch is a lunge and the kick a dropkick.
- In the air you can still punch and kick, and dash once per jump.
- Jump while pushing into a wall to jump off it. A ledge you reach catches you: push towards it or jump to climb, down to let go.

## Know this
- **The EXIT stays locked until every fighter is down.** It is usually at the right end; on a level with two floors, at the left end of the other floor.
- Going down is not the end: you get up at the start, and whoever you already dropped stays down.
- **Block crouched against a low strike**, and standing against one from the air.
- Every level sends at least one fighter who rushes you and one who hangs back and blocks.
- Clearing a level gives back almost half your health, not all of it.
