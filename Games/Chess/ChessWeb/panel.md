# Chess
> A friend across the table, or an engine at five strengths.

Play a friend on the same screen, or an engine that ranges from a beginner of about 800 to a master of about 2500. Every rule is in, from en passant to the fifty-move draw, with fifteen minutes on each clock. Step back through the game at any moment to see where it turned, or sit back and watch two engines play each other.

## How to start
- **2 Players**: two people take turns on one screen, White at the bottom.
- **Play as White** or **Play as Black**: pick the engine's strength and the game begins. As Black, the board turns so your pieces are at the bottom.
- **AI vs AI**: choose a strength for each side, then **Start**.

## Controls
- touch: **Drag** a piece, or **tap** it and then its square. The squares it can reach light up.
- keys: **Drag** a piece, or **click** it and then its square. The squares it can reach light up.
- {btnPrev} {btnNext} one move back or forward
- {btnFirst} {btnLast} the starting position, and back to the present
- keys: The keyboard does the same with [←] [→] and [Home] [End].
- keys: [R] restart the game
- keys: [N] back to the menu
- keys: [P] / [Space] pause
- When the game ends, **Rematch** (the same opponent and level) and **Menu** appear under the board.

## Know this
- **Each side has 15 minutes**, and only the clock of the side to move runs. Run out and you lose.
- A pawn that reaches the last rank always becomes a queen.
- Pausing stops your clock, but not the engine's while it is thinking.
- While you look back through the game nobody can move: {btnLast} brings you back.
