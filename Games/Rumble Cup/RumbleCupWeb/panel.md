# Rumble Cup
> Take the legs, take the ball: nobody calls a foul.

Five against five, with sliding tackles that floor a man and a special shot for every outfield player (a wrecking ball, a corkscrew, a quake lob). Each of the five pitches changes how the ball and the bodies move, from a mudbath to the moon, and a draw goes to a golden goal where the goals grow until somebody scores.

## Pick a match
- **CUP**: your team against three rivals in a row, quarter final to final, each on its home ground. Lose one and you can play it again.
- **FRIENDLY**: choose both teams, the pitch, the length (1:30 to 5:00) and how good the CPU is.
- Eight teams, each with its own way of playing and an edge on one surface: the team card says which.
- keys: In the menus the arrows pick and change a value, [Enter] confirms, [Esc] goes back.

## Controls
- touch: Your left thumb moves you, wherever it lands on the left half; pushed to the rim, it sprints.
- touch: <span class="k round">SHOOT</span> shoots (hold to charge). Without the ball it jumps, or reads VOLLEY or HEADER for a ball in the air.
- touch: <span class="k round">PASS</span> finds the mate you point at (hold for a lob).
- touch: <span class="k round">TACKLE</span> slides in. With the ball it reads BARGE and shoulders a defender off.
- touch: <span class="k round">SWITCH</span> takes the mate nearest the ball; <span class="k round">SPECIAL</span> glows when a press will fire it.
- keys: [W] [A] [S] [D] move (or the arrows)
- keys: [Shift] sprint (it spends stamina)
- keys: [K] shoot (hold to charge); without the ball, jump
- keys: [J] pass to the mate you point at (hold for a lob)
- keys: [L] sliding tackle; with the ball, a shoulder barge
- keys: [I] special shot
- keys: [Space] switch to the mate nearest the ball
- keys: [Z] [X] [C] [V] pass, shoot, tackle, special (the other hand's set)
- keys: [P] / [Esc] pause

## On the pitch
- **A sliding tackle that lands on the man on the ball wins it.** Near or far, it takes the legs of whoever is in its way. You have no punch, but the CPU's players may punch whoever just hit one of theirs.
- **A ball in the air is played first time.** At your waist, pass sends it to a mate, shoot volleys it and special fires your shot. Jump into a high ball to take it on the chest, and shoot again in the air for a header (pass for a headed pass).
- Your throw-ins, corners and keeper's kicks: the stick aims, pass plays it short and shoot sends it long.
- Heavy players shrug off what sends a light one flying, and a body sent flying takes down the light ones it lands on. Floored, you cannot be hit again: mash any button to get up sooner.

## The special
- The bar under your team's name fills with tackles, knockdowns, passes and shots, and with the beatings you take. It holds two charges.
- With a charge, special fires the shot of whoever has the ball. Each of the ten kinds of outfield player has a different one; keepers have none.

## The pitches
- **Green Park**: plain grass, the honest one.
- **Mudhole**: rain makes mud. The ball dies in it, feet slip, and tackles and falls pile it into mounds you can stand on to reach a high ball.
- **Glacier Rink**: ice. Nobody stops where they want, and the ball slides for ever and bounces off the boards: there is no out.
- **Clifftop**: a turning gale bends every ball in the air and blows light players and crates about.
- **Crater 9**: the moon. Low gravity, huge jumps, a floating ball and craters that swallow it.

## Tips
- Charging a shot, hold the stick up or down to pick the corner (the chevrons show where it goes); level, it aims itself.
- While you hold pass, the chevrons point to the mate it will reach.
