# Stone Crown
> From four villagers to a walled realm with an army.

Gather food, wood, gold and stone, spread your village across the map and raise an army to raze the rival's. Every choice costs something: another farm or another spearman, a wall now or the Crown Age sooner, while the rival builds a realm of its own.

## To win
- Leave the rival with **no Hearth and no villagers**. It wins the same way.

## Controls
- keys: **Click** selects, **drag** boxes several, [Shift]+click adds or removes, a **double click** takes every unit of that type on screen.
- keys: **Right-click** does what fits the spot: move, attack, gather, hunt, farm, build or repair, unload, shelter. With a building that trains, it sets the rally point; on the minimap it sends the selection there.
- keys: To build, pick it on the card and click its spot; [Shift] keeps placing. A wall is press, drag, release.
- keys: Hover a card button to see its cost and what it does. [Shift]+click on a unit's button queues five.
- keys: [W] [A] [S] [D] move the camera (or the arrows, or the screen edge). The wheel or [+] [-] zooms, the middle button drags.
- keys: [E] attack-move
- keys: [X] stop
- keys: [Q] / {btnIdle} next idle villager
- keys: [F] / {btnArmy} the whole army
- keys: [H] the Hearth
- keys: [Space] centre on the selection
- keys: [Shift] + [1–9] save a group; [1–9] calls it back, twice centres it
- keys: [Delete] twice: demolish the selected building
- keys: [P] pause · [Esc] cancel, or deselect
- touch: **Tap** a unit to select it, **two taps** for every unit of its type on screen. Hold a finger still a moment, then drag, to box several.
- touch: With units selected, tap the ground to move, an enemy to attack or a resource to work.
- touch: One finger drags the camera, two pinch to zoom; the minimap moves it too.
- touch: To build, pick the building, tap or drag its outline into place and confirm with the tick. A wall: tap its start, then its end, then the tick.
- touch: A card button shows its cost and what it does as you press it.
- touch: {btnIdle} next idle villager
- touch: {btnArmy} the whole army

## Growing
- **Food** from berry bushes, deer and farms, **wood** from trees, **gold** and **stone** from mines and quarries.
- Villagers carry every load to the Hearth or a **Storehouse**: build one beside the forest or a mine and the work comes in faster.
- Each **House** adds 5 population.
- The **Crown Age**, researched at the Hearth once two of Storehouse, Barracks and Archery Range stand, brings knights, the Forge's upgrades, rams and catapults, towers and stone walls.

## Fighting
- **Spearmen** stop knights, **knights** ride archers down, **archers** punish spearmen.
- The **ram** breaks buildings and walls and nothing else; the **catapult**'s stone hurts every enemy where it lands.
- The Hearth and towers shoot on their own. **Ring the bell** at the Hearth and villagers shelter in the nearest Hearth or Tower, adding arrows; **All clear** sends them back to work.
- **Walls**: lay a line of palisade (stone in the Crown Age), then select a segment to make it a gate that opens only for your people.
- The rival rarely attacks before minute 7 on Normal (5 on Hard, 9 on Easy): the clock is at the top.
