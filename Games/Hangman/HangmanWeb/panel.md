# Hangman
> Six wrong letters stand between him and the rope.

Guess the word one letter at a time before the figure on the gallows is complete. Every word comes with its category and a hint, hundreds of them in English and in Spanish, from animals to mythology, and you can hear how close you are: each miss lands lower and louder.

## Controls
- touch: Tap the letters on the keyboard under the word.
- keys: Type a letter, or click it on the keyboard under the word.
- [↻] deal another word
- [EN] / [ES] switch language (and deal a new word)
- touch: When a round ends, tap anywhere for the next word.
- keys: When a round ends, click or press [Enter] for the next word.

## Know this
- **Six misses and he hangs**: the head, the body, two arms and two legs.
- A right letter fills every place it appears. Each key turns green or red once tried, so no letter is spent twice.
- The Spanish words come without accents but keep the Ñ, which has its own key.
- Top left: your misses this word, and your wins and losses since you opened the game.
