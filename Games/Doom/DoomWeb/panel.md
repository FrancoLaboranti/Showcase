# Doom
> Five sublevels down, where the slag came alive.

Fight your way down a foundry buried under the city: find the coloured keys, reach the cargo lift and pull its lever to go deeper, until the last sublevel and the foundry goes cold. Six weapons against monsters of slag and iron that each fight their own way, and secrets on every floor for whoever looks closely.

## Controls
- touch: Left thumb walks: the stick appears where you touch, and at its rim you run. Right thumb drags to look.
- touch: {btnFire} fire (hold it and drag to aim as well)
- touch: {btnUse} open doors, pull levers
- touch: {btnReload} reload
- touch: {btnMap} map
- keys: Click the screen to capture the mouse: it looks, and the left button fires.
- keys: [W] [A] [S] [D] move and strafe (the arrows move and turn)
- keys: [Shift] run
- keys: [F] fire
- keys: [E] / [Space] / [Enter] open doors, pull levers
- keys: [R] reload
- keys: [Q] the last weapon (the wheel cycles them)
- keys: [Tab] / [M] map
- keys: [Esc] / [P] pause

## The arsenal
- You start with the knife and the riveter; the other four lie further down.
- [1] **Knife**: silent, up close, and it needs nothing.
- [2] **Riveter**: precise, and it never runs dry.
- [3] **Blunderbuss**: a fistful of scrap at arm's length.
- [4] **Stitcher**: a steady burst that spreads the longer you hold the trigger.
- [5] **Crucible**: a ball of slag that bursts over an area, and burns you too if you are close.
- [6] **Blowtorch**: a jet of fire that burns a pack at close range.

## Know them
- **Slag ghouls** crouch and glow before they leap: step aside.
- **Hooded stokers** keep their distance and throw slow fireballs.
- **Iron horseflies** dart in, bite and peel away.
- **Hammer-fisted brutes** paw the ground, then charge in a straight line. Dodge, and one that hits a wall is stunned and takes extra damage.
- **Bellringers** keep away and ring up more flies. **Kill them first.**

## Down here
- A coloured door wants the **key** of its colour, and some rooms lock behind you until the ambush inside is dead.
- **Secrets**: wall panels that open when you use them, and cracked walls that break under fire.
- Molten floors burn while you stand on them.
- **Dying** restarts the sublevel with what you carried into it. CONTINUE picks the run up exactly where you left it.
