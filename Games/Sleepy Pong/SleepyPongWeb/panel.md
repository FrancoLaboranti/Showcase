# Sleepy Pong
> The CPU dozes off. Make it pay.

Pong with two twists: **two points in a row set your paddle on fire**, and **the CPU falls asleep**. When the Zzz shows over its paddle it cannot move, and that is your window.

## The modes
- **One Player** and **Two Players** open a setup: the rival (One Player only), the points to win (5 to 40, or Practice, endless) and fire on or off.
- **Challenge**: the five rivals back to back, a match to 10 each. A loss ends the run, and the last rungs serve faster or force the fire on.
- **Bricks**: a wall over the net and two balls at once against the CPU. A coloured brick drops a power towards whoever broke it: catch it with your paddle.

## Controls
- touch: Hold ▲ ▼ in the bottom right corner to move your paddle. In Two Players the left player has a pair in the bottom left.
- touch: In the menu, tap an option. In the setup, tap the left or right half of a row to change it; ▶ PLAY starts.
- keys: [↑] [↓] move your paddle (and pick in the menu)
- keys: [W] [S] the left paddle, in Two Players
- keys: [←] [→] change a setup value
- keys: [Enter] confirm, or play on when a match ends
- keys: [P] pause; [Esc] back to the menu

## Know this
- **A paddle on fire** moves faster and hits harder. Concede a point and it goes out.
- Every return speeds the ball up, and the rally's sound climbs in pitch with it.
- Bricks' powers: orange sets your paddle on fire, green makes it faster, cyan sends an extra ball, and the rare silver shield saves one goal.
- Each rival plays its own way: Yellow sprays the ball and naps often, Sky is slow to react, Magenta is balanced, Green is fast and angles everything, and Black returns flat, barely sleeps and never catches fire on a streak.
- Your longest rally and best winning margin against each rival are kept (One Player and Challenge).
