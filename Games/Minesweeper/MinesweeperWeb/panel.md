# Minesweeper
> Read the numbers. Dig everything but the mines.

A number on an open cell says how many of the eight cells around it hide a mine; read the numbers together and they tell you where to dig and where to plant a flag. Classic is that pure puzzle; Gold Rush plants fewer mines, buries gold among them and starts a clock.

## Controls
- touch: Tap a cell to dig it.
- touch: Press and hold a cell to plant or lift a flag.
- keys: Click a cell to dig it.
- keys: Right click (or hold the left button) to plant or lift a flag.
- {btnMode} Classic or Gold Rush
- [★★] difficulty: each star means a bigger field and more mines
- touch: {btnReset} a new field
- keys: {btnReset} / [R] a new field
- keys: [M] sound on and off

## The field
- **The first dig is always safe**, and it always opens up a patch.
- A flagged cell cannot be dug, so a flag also guards against a slip of the finger.
- Open every cell that is not a mine to win. After a win or a blast, tap or click the field to play again.

## Gold Rush
- **Gold never opens by itself**: each nugget has to be dug out by hand. A golden **!** on an open cell means gold still lies next to it.
- Dig up every nugget before the clock runs out to win. Each one is worth the stars' multiplier (×1, ×2 or ×3), plus a point for every two seconds left.
- Hit a mine or run out of time and you keep only the nuggets, at ×1. Each difficulty keeps its own record.
