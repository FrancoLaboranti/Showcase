# Clock
> The time, in phosphor on black glass.

Your device's own time on a glowing analogue face, with the date and the time in digits above and below the centre. Pick one of seven colours and the clock changes its voice too: the tick and the hourly chime are tuned to that colour.

## Controls
- **Tap or click** anywhere to switch the second hand between **tick** (a jump each second) and **sweep** (a smooth glide).
- {btnSec} the same switch, from the bar
- {btnColor} next colour
- {btnAuto} auto colour: a new one every 2 seconds

## Listen
- A soft quartz tick every second, in both modes; sweep adds a quiet hum under it.
- A click marks each new minute, and a single glass chime each hour.
- With auto colour on, the tick changes pitch with every colour.
- The clock remembers its colour, its second hand, auto colour and the sound for next time.
