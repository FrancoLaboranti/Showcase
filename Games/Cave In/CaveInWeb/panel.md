# Cave-In
> Bomb your way down before the mine comes down on you.

Drop bombs that burst in a cross, blow open the crates and whatever crawls between them, and find the lift hidden under one of the crates to ride deeper. Four strata, from the shafts to the deep core, each end with a boss: the Borer, the Gloop Mother, the Cinderworm and the Foreman. Take too long and the mine starts caving in around you.

## How to start
- **Start at** a deeper stratum once you have beaten the boss above it.
- **Difficulty**: Easy gives 5 lives, slower creatures, 30 more seconds a depth and weaker bosses; Normal, 3 lives; Hard, 2 lives, faster creatures, 20 seconds less and tougher bosses.

## Controls
- touch: Put your thumb anywhere on the left half: the stick is born under it.
- touch: {btnBomb} drop a bomb
- touch: {btnDet} fire the detonator (it shows up while you carry one)
- keys: [W] [A] [S] [D] move (or the arrows)
- keys: [Space] / [J] drop a bomb
- keys: [K] fire the detonator
- keys: [P] / [Esc] pause, [M] sound
- keys: Hold a second direction and you turn into the next opening by yourself.

## Going down
- **The lift opens once every creature is gone.** Do not bomb it: it spits out more, and for a moment they cannot be hurt.
- On a boss's depth there is no crate and no clock: the lift appears in the middle once the boss and its creatures are down.
- When the clock runs out the mine caves in from the edges. A rock's shadow shows where it will land, and the rock hurts.

## Bombs
- **The glow on the floor is exactly where a blast will reach**, and it burns brighter as the fuse runs out.
- Stone stops a blast; a crate breaks and stops it too.
- **Only your own cell counts**: a blast that does not reach it cannot hurt you, and the grey smoke it leaves is harmless.
- A blast that reaches another bomb sets it off. In one blast, chain included, each creature after the first scores double: x2, x4, up to x16.

## Know the fauna
- The **mole** stops with a red "!" when it smells you, then comes straight at you. A bomb in its way stops it: use it as bait.
- A **slime** never walks into a glow, and splits in two when it dies.
- The **beetle** charges down any row or column it sees you in. The **wisp** floats through crates to where you were a moment ago.
- The **golem** takes three blasts, and leaves its patch only when you come close.
