# Newton's Cradle
> Lift one ball. Watch the far one answer.

The classic desk toy: lift a sphere, let it fall, and the impulse runs through the row and kicks out the one at the far end. Lift two or three together and the same number swings out the other side. There is nothing to win; it is there to be played with and watched.

## Controls
- **Drag a sphere aside and let go** to set the others going.
- To lift two or three, drag the second or third sphere from an end outwards: the ones beyond it come along.
- {btnPlus} / {btnMinus} add or take away a sphere, from 1 to 10 (hold to keep going)
- {btnGhost} ghost mode: they pass through each other instead of colliding
- {btnHold} freeze: everything stops in place, and a sphere you lift stays up there. Press it again and they all fall.
- {btnReset} restart: they hang back at rest

## Tips
- With the sound on, every clack is a hit of a real recorded cradle, and the harder they meet, the harder the hit you hear.
- Look past the cradle: the window goes from day to night and back about once a minute.
