# Simon Says
> Watch, listen, repeat. Then one step more.

The disc lights up a sequence, each colour with a note of its own, and you play it back. Every round you get right adds one step to the end and plays the whole thing a little faster, so the only question is how far your memory holds.

## How to play
- touch: Tap the centre (**START**) and watch. When the header says **YOUR TURN**, tap the quarters in the same order.
- keys: Click the centre (**START**) and watch. When the header says **YOUR TURN**, click the quarters in the same order.
- A quarter sounds for as long as you hold it, and counts when you let go.
- There is no clock on your turn: take your time.

## Know this
- **You have three lives**, the dots under the header. A mistake costs one and plays the same sequence again; the third ends the run.
- After a game over the centre reads **MENU**: press it, then **START** to play again. Your best score is kept.

## Tips
- **Each colour always plays the same note, and the four make a chord.** Hum the sequence back and you remember it by ear as well as by sight, which is how the original toy worked.
