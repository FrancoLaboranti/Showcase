# Lanternvale
> One village, five wild zones, and a blight to end.

Take a quest in the village, walk out of the gate and fight through meadow, forest, crags, a dark cave and the marsh beyond. Bring the loot home, sell it, buy better gear and push on: every main quest opens the next road, and bosses stand in the way.

## Controls
- touch: Touch the lower left of the screen and a stick appears under your thumb.
- touch: {btnAtk} attack (hold to keep swinging)
- touch: {btnDodge} dodge
- touch: {btnSk0} {btnSk1} {btnSk2} {btnSk3} skills, as they unlock
- touch: {btnPot} drink a potion
- touch: [TALK] [OPEN] [READ] pops up near something you can use; tapping the person or the chest works too
- touch: {btnBag} {btnQuests} {btnMap} bag, quests and map
- keys: [W] [A] [S] [D] walk (or the arrows)
- keys: [Space] / [J] attack (hold to keep swinging)
- keys: [K] / [Shift] dodge
- keys: [1–4] skills, as they unlock
- keys: [Q] drink a potion
- keys: [E] / [Enter] talk, open, pick up, read
- keys: [I] [L] [M] bag, quests and map
- keys: [P] / [Esc] pause
- keys: In a conversation, [Space] moves on and the number keys pick an answer. The mouse works every menu.

## On the road
- **Attacks aim themselves** at the nearest monster, and three quick swings make a combo. A dodge makes you untouchable for an instant.
- **Red marks on the ground** are swings and slams about to land: step out. Lunges and throws give no mark, so watch the monster wind up.
- Skills unlock at levels 2, 4, 7 and 10 and recharge by themselves. A level up heals you completely.
- Drinking picks the smallest potion that heals you fully, or your biggest. Sister Wren, in the village, heals you for free.
- Gear comes in <span class="rc0">common</span>, <span class="rc1">uncommon</span>, <span class="rc2">rare</span> and <span class="rc3">epic</span>. Champions with a golden rim always drop some. Any merchant buys what you carry, except quest items.
- If you fall, you wake up in the village a tenth of your gold lighter. The game saves by itself.
- The arrow at the edge of the screen points at the quest with the **orange diamond**: the main one, unless you **Follow this one** on another in the quest log.

## Tips
- A Return Scroll from Pipette takes you home from anywhere.
- Once you have helped Elder Maren, the bounty board in the village offers hunts for gold and XP, one after another.
- Chests, hidden paths and a golden hare reward a look off the trail. Monsters come back after a while, bosses after five minutes.
