Snake

A sea snake on a dark reef. Eat stars to grow and level up. Every level lets you pick an upgrade. Leaving the lake drains your health continuously.
Desktop
  • Move the mouse and the snake turns toward the pointer.
  • Hold any mouse button to sprint. It burns turbo, which refills when you let go.
  • Pause and Restart in the top-right cluster.
Mobile
  • Joystick at the bottom left steers.
  • Sprint (hold), the button at the bottom right, accelerates while held.
  • Two fingers anywhere on the canvas also sprint, without reaching for the button.
In the water
  • Jellyfish: a shield for a few seconds. Seahorse: extra speed for a while.
  • Mines hit hard and tear a piece off you.
  • Enemy snakes: lunge at them to bite.
  • Bosses every few levels. Each one has a window in which it can actually be hurt; outside it the armour absorbs nearly everything.
