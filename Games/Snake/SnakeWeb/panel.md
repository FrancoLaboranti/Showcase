# Snake
> Eat, grow, and out-bite every snake in the lake.

A sea snake over a dark reef, in a lake full of rival snakes. Eat stars to grow and heal, sprint at a rival's head to lunge and bite, and pick an upgrade card at every level to shape how you fight. Every fifth level a boss arrives, each with its own weakness to find.

## Controls
- keys: Move the mouse and the snake swims toward the pointer.
- keys: Hold any mouse button to sprint. It burns turbo, which refills when you let go.
- keys: [P] pause
- touch: The stick appears where your thumb lands in the bottom third of the screen.
- touch: {btnSprint} sprint while held. It is also the turbo gauge: it drains as you sprint and refills when you let go.
- touch: Two fingers anywhere also sprint.

## The fight
- **Only heads can be bitten**, yours included. Sprint with a rival's head in front of you and you lunge; the bite lands only if your head reaches its head.
- Rivals fight the same way, and some grow extra heads that bite on their own. The longer you last, the busier the lake.
- Kills level you up far faster than stars. A bigger snake bites harder, but turns slower.
- **Leaving the lake burns your health** for as long as you stay out.

## In the water
- **Stars** and upgrade cards share one ladder of rarity: white, green, blue, violet, gold, red, each worth more than the last.
- **Jellyfish**: a few seconds of shield. **Seahorses**: a burst of speed.
- **Mines** hit hard and tear a piece off you.

## The bosses
- **Puffer**: bite it while it is deflated. Inflated, its spines hurt to touch and shrug off your bites.
- **Stone**: it prowls the reef and stings with venom that stops your healing. Bite it while it recovers from a strike.
- **Angler**: it darkens the water. Bite it with its mouth open; dodge its straight charge, or bite into it for a stun and double damage.
