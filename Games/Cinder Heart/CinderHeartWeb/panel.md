# Cinderheart
> A stolen sun, unlikely allies, and a stair into the dark.

Wren robbed the wrong reliquary and woke up with the last ember of a dead sun burning in her chest. Fight across six zones with the guard sent to arrest her, the back-alley surgeon who stitched her up, and whoever joins on the way. Zones bend the rules of combat and end in a boss; victories bring loot, and every level a point for a skill tree of abilities and upgrades.

## The road
- Pick the difficulty before **New campaign**: it holds for the whole campaign. The game saves as you go.
- **A defeat costs nothing.** Everyone is healed after every fight, so try again or change something at the Camp.

## Controls
- touch: Tap a unit to aim at it. Tap an ability on the ring to read it, then again (or **Use**) to act.
- touch: Tap the empty stage to close the ring.
- keys: Click a unit to aim at it. Click an ability to read it, then again (or **Use**) to act.
- keys: [1–9] pick an ability, again to act (1 is the basic attack, the last Guard)
- keys: [←] [→] / [Tab] move the aim
- keys: [Enter] / [Space] act, or the next line of a dialogue
- keys: [1] [2] answer in a dialogue
- keys: [Esc] cancel
- keys: [P] pause, with the battle speed (1x or 2x) and the battle log
- touch: {btnPause} pause, with the battle speed (1x or 2x) and the battle log
- Tap or click anything to read it: a unit or its plate, a status, a turn-order portrait. On your turn a unit takes two taps: the first one aims.

## In battle
- **Speed, not rounds.** The portraits along the top are the turns to come, and faster units come up more often. A picked ability shows how it would change them.
- **Ember**, the flames under each unit, pays for the strong abilities (finishers spend it all); basic attacks and Guard give 1. A foe with a full row is about to hit hard.
- A red **!** in the turn order is a charged attack, on the turn it lands. Stun the attacker, shield the target or Guard.
- **Guard** halves the damage until your next turn, gives 1 Ember and brings that turn sooner.
- A big number on an ability is its cooldown, in your turns.
- From the second zone on, a zone rule applies (top left: tap it). A purple token in the turn order is the zone acting.

## At the Camp
- **Gear**: a weapon, armour and a charm each. Most pieces trade one strength for another, so compare before you equip.
- **Abilities**: drag one onto a place of the ring (3, then 4 from level 4), or off it; that is where it sits in battle.
- **Tree**: a point per level, reset for free between battles. Wren can swear only one Oath, and it decides what she becomes.
- Three fight at a time, Wren always among them. When more have joined, choose here who sits out.
