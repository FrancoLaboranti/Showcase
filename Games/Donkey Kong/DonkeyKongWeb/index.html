Donkey Kong

Climb to the top past the barrels the gorilla rolls down. You get three lives and a BONUS that counts down. Jumping over a barrel scores, and chaining jumps over different barrels inside a short window multiplies it, up to x5.
Desktop
  • ← → or A D walk. ↑ ↓ or W S climb ladders.
  • Space jumps. Enter starts. Esc goes back to the menu.
Mobile
  • Walk left and Walk right move; Climb up and Climb down take ladders.
  • Tap the screen to jump.
On the way up
  • The hammer floats above a girder, so the only way to take it is by jumping. For eight seconds it smashes whatever it touches, but while you hold it you cannot jump or climb.
  • Rivets: walking over one pops it, and popping all eight opens the warp.
  • Collectibles: three per level, and taking all three pays a set bonus.
