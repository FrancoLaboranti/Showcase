# Donkey Kong
> Climb through the barrels. Pauline is at the top.

The 1981 arcade climb: the gorilla hurls barrels down the girders, fire creeps out of the oil drum, and you run, jump and climb to reach Pauline before the bonus runs dry. After the first level every board is reshuffled, and you choose how hard it hits back.

## How to start
- **PLAY** starts at level 1. **DAILY CHALLENGE** plays the day's boards, the same all day, with a record of its own.
- The swatches on the menu dress you in a new colour. More unlock as your points pile up, game after game.

- keys: [Space] jump

## The climb
- **A fall from higher than your own jump costs a life.** Take the ladders down too, never the edge.
- **The BONUS counts down**: reach Pauline and you keep what is left; if it hits zero, you lose a life. You start with three.
- A **broken ladder** only takes you as far as the break, but barrels and fire come down it all the same.
- The **hammer** floats above a girder: jump to grab it. For eight seconds it smashes barrels and fire, but you cannot jump or climb.
- **Jumping a barrel scores**, and chaining jumps over different barrels multiplies it, up to x5.

## Further up
- From level 2 each level opens with a choice: **CALM**, or a hazard (FAST BARRELS, BARREL RAIN, CLIMBERS, INFERNAL) that is harder and pays more. Tap one, or pick with the arrows.
- **RAGE!** flashes once a level as a warning: then, for six seconds, the barrels come faster and closer together.
- A **blue barrel** is faster and never takes a ladder, and jumping it is worth more.
- Every fourth level has eight **rivets**: walk over them all for a bonus at the top. The others hide Pauline's **hat, purse and umbrella**; take all three for a bonus.
