Fireworks

A sky to launch fireworks into. No score and no end. The show is yours to run!
Desktop
  • Click and drag to launch a rocket where you aim.
  • Auto show keeps launching on its own; ×5 sends five at once.
  • Burst one at a time (hold it down) pops the rockets still climbing, one by one; Burst everything in the air pops them together.
Mobile
  • Touch and drag to launch where you aim. The bar buttons behave the same as on desktop.
