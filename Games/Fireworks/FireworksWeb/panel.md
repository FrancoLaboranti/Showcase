# Fireworks
> Point at the night sky and set it alight.

Tap or click anywhere and a rocket climbs from that spot. Nine kinds of burst, from peonies and rings to willows, palms and crossettes that break a second time, and every 25th launch is a salvo: five to seven rockets bursting alike, in one colour scheme. No score and no end: the show runs as long as you want it.

## Controls
- touch: **Touch** to launch; **hold and drag** to keep launching, one stream per finger.
- keys: **Click** to launch; **hold and drag** to keep launching.
- {btnAuto} auto show: rockets go up on their own
- [×5] while lit, every launch sends five, the auto show's too
- {btnBlowOne} hold it to burst the rockets in the air, one by one
- {btnBlowAll} burst everything in the air at once

## Good to know
- About one rocket in ten is a dud and falls dark. {btnBlowAll} still sets it off on the way down.
- For a finale, light ×5 and the auto show together: five rockets at a time, all on their own.
