# Tic-Tac-Toe
> Three in a row, against a friend or a CPU that slips.

The oldest duel on paper, played with glass marks on a lacquered board, and the notes climb as the board fills. Take on a friend on the same screen, or the CPU: it never misses a win or a block, but now and then it slips, so it can be beaten.

## How to play
- Get three of your marks in a line (a row, a column or a diagonal) before the other side does. Who opens alternates every round.
- {btnMode} picks the game: **2 players**, **you as X** against the CPU, or **you as O**. Against the CPU its icon shows your mark. Changing it clears the board and the score.

## Controls
- touch: Tap a cell to place your mark.
- keys: Click a cell to place your mark.
- {btnReset} new round
- {btnScores} clears the X, O and draws tally, and the board

## Tip
- The CPU blocks every line you threaten, so beat it with a fork: one mark that opens two lines at once.
