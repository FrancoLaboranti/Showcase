# Kung-Fu Master
> Five floors to the top. Every blow warns you first.

Fight your way up a tower, hall by hall, as grapplers, knife throwers, brutes and leapers come at you from both sides. Every attack has a tell: read it, duck or jump, and hit back while they recover. A guardian waits at the foot of each staircase, and the Grandmaster at the top.

## The climb
- Beat enough of a floor's fighters (the red **ENEMY** bar counts them down) and its guardian comes out; beat him to take the stairs. If the **TIME** runs out, you lose a life.
- Every fighter you beat gives a little energy back, and passing the middle of a floor saves your place.
- Three lives, and one more at 30,000, 80,000 and 150,000 points. A continue restarts the floor with the score back at zero.
- After the Grandmaster the tower starts again, faster.

## Controls
- touch: The joystick is born where your left thumb lands: sideways to walk, up to jump, down to crouch. Push it out past its ring to run.
- touch: Right thumb: <span class="k round hi">HIGH</span> on top, <span class="k round lo">LOW</span> and <span class="k round ki">KICK</span> underneath.
- touch: {btnBlock} block, while you hold it
- keys: [A] [D] walk (or the arrows); hold [Shift] to run
- keys: [W] / [Space] jump (hold a direction to jump forward)
- keys: [S] crouch
- keys: [J] [K] [L] high punch, low punch, kick (or [Z] [X] [C])
- keys: [V] / [B] block, while you hold it
- keys: [P] / [Esc] pause

## The moves
- <span class="k round hi">HIGH</span> is fast and short, at head height: small fighters duck under it. Crouched, it punches from the crouch.
- <span class="k round lo">LOW</span> crouches as it hits, so it also ducks a high knife.
- <span class="k round ki">KICK</span> reaches further but is slower; crouched it is a sweep along the floor, in the air a flying kick.
- Out of a run, down with LOW or KICK slides in along the floor.
- **Block** stops what comes from the front (crouch to stop the low ones too). It still costs a little energy, and a few guardian attacks go through it.

## Know this
- Crouch under a high knife, jump over a low one, or hit it in flight to knock it away.
- **Grabbed?** Shake left and right or mash the buttons to break free.
- A leaper's shadow marks where he lands. A jar rattling on its rope breaks into a snake unless you punch it as it falls.
- Guardians show every attack before they throw it, and none can be stunned for long: land a few blows, then get ready to dodge.

## Tips
- Time left on the clock turns into points, and a guardian beaten without taking a hit is worth 5,000 more.
- The Grandmaster's blue stance is a counter: wait it out.
