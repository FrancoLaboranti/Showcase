Kung-Fu Master

Climb the five floors of the tower. Fighters come at you from both sides, every floor ends with its guardian at the foot of the stairs, and the master waits at the top. Clear each floor before the TIME runs out.
Desktop
  • AD or ←→ walk, W or ↑ jumps (hold a direction to jump forward), S or ↓ crouches.
  • J high punch, K low punch, L kick (or ZXC). P or Esc pauses.
Mobile
  • The joystick is born where you touch the left half: sideways to walk, up to jump, down to crouch.
  • Three buttons on the right: HIGH on top, LOW and KICK underneath.
Fighting
  • HIGH is fast and short, at head height: small fighters duck under it. LOW crouches as it hits. KICK reaches further but is slower; crouched it is a sweep, in the air a flying kick.
  • Crouch under high knives, jump over low ones, and hit a knife in flight to knock it away. If someone grabs you, shake the joystick or mash the buttons to break free.
  • Guardians show every attack before they throw it: learn the pose, dodge, and hit back while they recover.
