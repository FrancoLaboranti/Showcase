# Bloom
> Nothing to beat, nothing to lose: just your garden.

Plant tulips, daffodils and hydrangeas, water them, and gather the petals their blooms fill with. Petals buy more land, and the land takes paths, ponds, benches, lanterns and a fountain, painted in any colour or in designs you draw yourself. Day turns to night while you work, and a plant never dies.

## Your garden
- **Petals pay for land.** A bloom fills with them slowly; gather them and buy the next plot with **Expand** (in Land, or tap your petals at the top).
- New plants and objects unlock as you grow, place and paint. The **journal**, the book at the top, says what each one asks for.
- With enough petals, **Sprinklers** in the Water tray water the whole garden for you, even while you are away.

## Controls
- touch: **Tap** soil or an empty pot to plant the seed picked below; tap a bloom to gather its petals.
- touch: **Drag** across soil, plants or blooms to plant, water or gather several at once.
- touch: **Hold** a plant for its card: water, gather or uproot.
- touch: Drag anywhere else to look around, and **pinch** to zoom.
- keys: Click works like a tap and dragging like a finger; **hold** a plant for its card. The wheel zooms.
- keys: [1–6] the six tools, in the order below
- keys: [+] / [-] zoom
- keys: [P] / [Esc] pause, [M] sound

## The six tools
- **Plant**: pick a seed, then tap soil or an empty pot.
- **Water**: tap or drag across thirsty plants.
- **Paint**: pick a colour or a design and tap objects; it costs nothing. **New** opens the studio, to draw a design of your own.
- **Decorate**: pick an object and tap free ground. Tap a placed one to turn it or put it away.
- **Land**: lay grass, soil, paths or water.
- **Move**: tap a plant or an object, then the spot where it goes.

## Growing
- **Shade lies along the back walls**; the rest is sun. A seed card's sun or cloud says what it likes, and in the wrong light a plant grows slower.
- A watering lasts about a minute. A thirsty plant droops and slows down, but it never dies.
- The garden keeps growing for up to a quarter of an hour after you close it.

## Tips
- Tap the clock to skip to the next part of the day. At night the lanterns light up and the fireflies come out.
- In the studio's mixer, red and blue make violet, red and yellow orange, blue and yellow green.
