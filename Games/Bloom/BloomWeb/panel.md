# Bloom

Plant, water and harvest flowers for their colors, mix them, and paint a garden that is yours. Nothing is ever lost. This whole file is a PROPOSAL that Franco edits before it is published.

## Controls
- touch: **Tap** a bed to plant, a plant to look at it, a bloom to pick it.
- touch: **Drag** across beds or plants to plant or water several at once, and to move a decoration.
- keys: Click works like a tap, and dragging like a finger.
- keys: [1] [2] [3] [4] Plant, Water, Paint, Decorate
- keys: [P] / [Esc] pause
- keys: [M] sound on and off
- touch: {btnPause} pause

## Good to know
- Red and yellow flowers like **sun**, blue likes **shade**; grass and bushes are happy anywhere. In the wrong light a plant grows slower.
- A watering lasts about a minute. A thirsty plant droops and grows slower, but it never dies.
- A bloom you looked after gives one more pigment.
- Red + blue = violet, red + yellow = orange, blue + yellow = green. Each coat of paint uses one pigment.
- The lock on the gate says what the next part of the garden asks for.
- The garden keeps growing for a while after you close it.
