Crazy Tanks

A race between tanks on a track generated fresh every time. Finish the laps first, and you can shoot the others. A hit sends the other tank into a spin.
Desktop
  • Arrow keys or WASD drive, in the menu as well. Space fires a shell, same as Fire. Shift uses nitro, same as Nitro.
  • Enter confirms in the menu. Esc goes back. Pause in the corner.
Mobile
  • Joystick on the left drives.
  • Fire and Nitro are the buttons on the right.
Nitro runs out: pick up orbs on the track to refill it, or drift for real and it recharges on its own, a little per drift. Each biome has its own grip: on snow the tank slides. The track rattle changes with the surface before you feel the slide.
