# Crazy Tanks
> Sixteen tanks, a new track every race, and bombs.

Race fifteen rival tanks on a circuit generated fresh for every race, across six biomes from a green park to lava and a rainy night. Grab nitro off the road, ride a rival's wake to slingshot past it, and put a bomb into whoever is ahead.

## Pick a race
- **New Race** is one race: choose the laps (3, 5 or 10), how good the rivals are (Very Easy to Very Hard), the biome and your tank.
- **Tournament** is six races, one per biome. Each pays points by place, 16 for the winner down to 1, and the standings follow every race.

## Controls
- touch: Left thumb drives: point the stick where you want to go, and push it further to go faster.
- touch: {btnFire} fire a bomb (one every two seconds)
- touch: {btnNitro} nitro (hold)
- touch: In the menus, tap the left half of a row for the previous value and the right half for the next.
- keys: [↑] [↓] accelerate, brake and reverse
- keys: [←] [→] steer
- keys: [W] [A] [S] [D] the same as the arrows, in the menus too
- keys: [Space] fire a bomb (one every two seconds)
- keys: [Shift] nitro (hold)
- keys: [Enter] / [Esc] confirm, go back (the mouse works too: click a row's left or right half)
- keys: [P] pause

## On the track
- **A bomb spins a tank out** and nearly stops it. It flies straight off your nose, so line up first.
- **Nitro runs out.** You start with half a bar; the orbs on the road refill it (a magenta one gives double), up to two bars. An orb nobody takes fades and turns up somewhere else.
- Tuck in right behind another tank for a second, then pull out of its wake: the tow shoots you past.
- A slick patch on the road takes your steering for a moment, and on snow the snowmen throw snowballs at you.
- Some tracks have portals: drive off one edge and you come back in at the other.
- **Back ({btnBack} or [Esc]) leaves a race at once**, with no confirmation; in a tournament, every point so far is lost.

## Tips
- Every biome grips differently: the desert holds your line, snow slides wide, and the rainy night is the fastest of all.
- Medals at the end of a race: **Clean** (no bomb hit you), **Sniper** (three hits landed) and **Efficient** (under half a bar of nitro spent). The menu keeps the count.
