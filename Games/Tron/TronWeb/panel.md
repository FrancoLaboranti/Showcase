# Tron
> Cut them off before they cut you off.

Your light cycle never stops, and it leaves a solid wall of light behind it. Turn at the right moment to box the CPU riders in, slip through the gaps they leave, and be the last cycle still running.

## The grid
- Hit any trail, **your own included**, and you are out.
- **The edges wrap**: ride off one side and you come back on the other, and so do the trails. There are no walls.
- You ride the **cyan** cycle against one to three CPU riders. A round is yours only if you are the last one left.
- The menu sets the riders and the length of the set: a single round, or the best of 3, 5 or 7.

## Controls
- A cycle only turns, a quarter turn per press: no throttle, no brake.
- keys: [←] turn left
- keys: [↓] turn right
- keys: [Enter] next round
- keys: [Esc] back to the menu
- keys: In the menu, [↑] [↓] pick a row, [←] [→] change it, [Enter] plays.
- touch: Tap the left or right half of the screen to turn that way.
- touch: {btnLeft} {btnRight} turn left, turn right
- touch: In the menu, tap a row to change it, then PLAY.

## Tip
- Listen: the engine climbs as a trail closes in ahead of you.
