# Tron
> Cut them off before they cut you off.

Your light cycle never stops, and it leaves a solid wall of light behind it. Turn at the right moment to box the CPU riders in, slip through the gaps they leave, and be the last cycle still running.

## The grid
- Hit any trail, **your own included**, and you are out.
- **The edges wrap**: ride off one side and you come back on the other, and so do the trails.
- You ride the **cyan** cycle against one to three CPU riders, in a single round or the best of 3, 5 or 7.

## Controls
- A cycle only turns, a quarter turn per press: no throttle, no brake.
- keys: [←] turn left
- keys: [↓] turn right
- keys: A click on the left or right half of the screen turns that way too.
- keys: [Enter] next round, [P] pause, [Esc] back to the menu
- keys: In the menu, [↑] [↓] pick a row, [←] [→] change it, [Enter] plays.
- pad: [LB] or the D-pad's left turns left, [RB] or its right turns right, and the stick does the same
- pad: [A] next round, [B] back to the menu once the round is over
- pad: [Start] pause, [Select] back to the menu
- pad: In the menu, the D-pad picks a row and changes it, [A] plays.
- touch: Tap the left or right half of the screen to turn that way, or press {btnLeft} {btnRight}.

## Tip
- Listen: the engine climbs as a trail closes in ahead of you.
