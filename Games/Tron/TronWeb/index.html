Tron

Light cycles that leave a solid trail. Hit any trail, including your own, and you are de-rezzed. The field wraps: leave through one edge and you come back through the opposite one, and so do the trails. A round is a leg. The set is the real result.
Desktop
  • ← and ↓ turn. There is no forward or brake: a cycle only turns.
  • Enter continues after a round. Esc goes back to the menu.
  • In the menu, ↑ ↓ pick the row and ← → change the value: how many colours race and how many rounds the set is.
Mobile
  • Tap the left or right half of the screen to turn that way.
  • Turn left and Turn right in the bar do the same.
  • Tap a menu row to change its value, then PLAY.
