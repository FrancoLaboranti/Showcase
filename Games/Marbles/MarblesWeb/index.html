Marbles

A box of marbles with real physics. There is no goal here but to mess around.
Desktop
  • Drag a marble and let go to throw it. The faster the gesture, the harder the throw.
  • Q pins the marble you are holding in mid-air, same as Pin.
  • Space makes them all jump, same as Jump.
  • Add a ball / Remove a ball change how many there are. Wall mode cycles the four: closed, no sides, no ceiling or floor and none at all. Restart puts everything back.
Mobile
  • Drag a marble and let go to throw it.
  • Jump, Pin, Wall mode, Add a ball, Remove a ball and Restart are the bar buttons, left to right.
The bar chart at the top left is the ten fastest marbles in px/s.
