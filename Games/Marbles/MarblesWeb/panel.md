# Marbles
> Throw them, pile them, pin them in mid-air.

A box of glass marbles with real physics, and the sound of real ones: every click and roll is a recording. Fling them at each other, pin one in mid-air and let the rest pile onto it, or open the walls and watch them fall out one side and come back in the other. No goal and no score, just the marbles.

## Controls
- touch: Drag a marble and let go to throw it: the faster the flick, the harder the throw. Each finger can hold a marble of its own.
- keys: Drag a marble and let go to throw it: the faster the flick, the harder the throw.
- touch: {btnJump} every marble jumps (or shake the phone)
- keys: {btnJump} / [Space] every marble jumps
- touch: {btnPin} pins the marble you are holding: tap it with another finger. Hold a pinned one and tap again to free it.
- keys: [Q] pins the marble you are dragging; grab a pinned one and press [Q] again to free it.
- {btnWall} wall mode: closed, no sides, no ceiling or floor, or no walls at all (the icon shows which)
- {btnPlus} / {btnMinus} add or remove a marble (hold to keep going)
- {btnReset} fresh marbles, as many as there are, with every pin freed
- keys: [M] sound on or off

## Know this
- **A pinned marble does not budge**: the others bounce off it and pile onto it, however many there are.
- Through an open wall a marble leaves the box and comes back in on the other side.
- The big marbles are the heavy ones: they shove the small ones aside.

## Tips
- It starts with one: hold {btnPlus} to pour in more, up to 100.
- Pin a few high up, then drop the rest onto them.
- Press on an empty spot and sweep: you catch the first marble you pass over.
