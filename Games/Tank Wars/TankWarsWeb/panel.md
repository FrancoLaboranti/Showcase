# Tank WARS
> Every wreck pays for a deadlier tank.

Hold out against fifty waves of enemy tanks, from laser-sighted snipers and rammers to bosses that breed and teleport. Every wreck drops gold for the shop and the odd lootbox offers a new power, so the tank that reaches wave 50 is not the one that started.

## The run
- Pick a **tank** and any **mutators**, then **PLAY**. Every tank spends the same power differently, and each mutator is a trade: hover one (hold it, on a touch screen) to read it.
- **Hardcore** brings the same waves sooner and tougher, with more elites and mines and fewer lootboxes.
- **50 waves** and a **Final Boss**. Kill it and the run goes on in **Endless** until you die.
- Death ends the run, its upgrades and its powers. New tank colours unlock at wave 20, five bosses in one run, the Final Boss, 50,000 points and Endless ×5.

## Controls
- touch: Left thumb drives. Right thumb moves the sight and **fires while it is down**; the sight clings to targets near it.
- touch: {btnMissile} homing missile
- touch: {btnBoost} boost (hold)
- touch: {btnShop} shop (the game waits)
- touch: Tap the **rage bar**, under your health, when it is full.
- keys: [W] [A] [S] [D] drive (or the arrows)
- keys: Mouse aims, the left button fires and the right one sends a homing missile.
- keys: [Shift] boost (hold)
- keys: [Q] rage, when the bar is full
- keys: [Tab] shop (the game waits)
- keys: [P] pause

## Staying alive
- **The shield rises by itself** against shells and missiles until its charge runs dry; then it is down for a few seconds.
- **Mines, exploding tanks and rams go through it.** Mines hurt enemies too, and the minimap does not show them.
- A beam and a ring closing on you mean a missile is locking on: break the line of fire or let the shield take it.
- **4 missiles**, one back every 10 seconds; they home on the target ahead.
- **Rage** fills as you deal and take damage. Full, it gives **12 seconds** of invulnerability, speed, rapid fire and unlimited missiles.

## Loot
- **Coins** drop from wrecks and crates: drive close before they fade.
- **Lootboxes** open with ten shots or one missile. Most hold coins, a green one heals (even past full) and a purple one lets you pick **1 of 3 powers** for the run.
- Quick kills build a score **combo** up to ×5; elites (a coloured band) and **BOUNTY** targets pay more.

## Tips
- Boost is free: hold it to leave a crossfire or reach a lootbox before it vanishes.
- The floor's tint leans towards the colour of the enemies coming next.
