# Loop
> Cross your own thread: everything inside takes the hit.

You are a marble trailing a thread of light over a clock face, and chess pieces come at you along lanes they show you before they move. Bat orbs into them with the thread, close loops around them, and survive twelve hours: each one brings a rule you choose, and the twelfth brings the Watchmaker.

## The thread
- **Cross your own thread** to close a loop, and everything inside takes the hit. **A tight loop hits harder; a big one scores more.**
- The thread bats orbs like a paddle, and a batted orb turns <span class="g">CHARGED</span> and hurts the pieces. Swing your head into it and it flies the way you are moving.
- A third of the way into each hour a <span class="g">diamond</span> lights up in each of the nine sectors: loop one to claim it. **Three in a row sets off a FRENZY** (once an hour): nothing can hurt you, the pieces are dragged to you and break at a touch for double points, and you win back a third of your health.

## The clock
- One sweep of the hand is an **hour**. It chimes at 3, 6, 9 and 12, and each chime sends a shockwave out from the centre.
- From the second hour on, you choose one **rule** of three. The bolder the rule, the more your <span class="g">STREAK</span> multiplier grows, and it never comes back down.
- Your score buys **cards**: each time it passes the next mark you pick one of three. Each card is an upgrade and a playing card, and five make a **poker hand**: the better the hand, the bigger its bonus. With five in hand, a new card replaces one, or you keep your hand.
- A **pawn** that reaches the centre of the face promotes to a stronger piece.

## Controls
- touch: Put your left thumb down anywhere on the left half: the stick is born there.
- touch: [DASH] lunge: nothing can hurt you, you hit what you cross, and the thread pulls straight (the quickest way to close a loop)
- touch: [PULSE] a shockwave that shoves everything away
- touch: Tap the strip of cards to see your hand and the poker hands.
- keys: [W] [A] [S] [D] move, or the arrows
- keys: [Space] / [Shift] lunge: nothing can hurt you, you hit what you cross, and the thread pulls straight (the quickest way to close a loop)
- keys: [E] / [Q] pulse: a shockwave that shoves everything away
- keys: [H] your cards and the poker hands (the game waits)
- keys: [1–3] pick a rule or a card; with a full hand, [1–5] the card to replace, or [Q] to keep your hand
- keys: [P] pause
- keys: [T] the tuning panel, an extra toy: live sliders for the game's numbers.

## Tips
- With a piece on top of you, close a small loop for damage; when the face is quiet, draw a big one for points.
- **TEST RUN** cannot kill you: the place to learn the rules and the cards.
