Cinderheart

A turn-based party RPG. Lead Wren, a thief with a stolen sun in her chest, up the Stair of Morning: six zones, a story, events, and a boss at the end of each.
Turns
  • Units act by speed, not in rounds: the portraits at the top are the next ten turns, and faster units appear more often.
  • Pick an ability and the turn order previews it: Haste, Slow, Delay and quick actions move the portraits before you commit.
  • A red ! is a charged attack that lands on that unit's next turn; a stun interrupts it. The purple token is the zone itself acting.
Ember
  • The flames under every name. Basic attacks and Guard give 1, some abilities give more. Strong abilities spend it; finishers spend all of it.
  • Enemies have Ember too: a full row means their big attack is close. Some abilities steal it.
Abilities
  • Tap an ability, then a target. Abilities that hit everyone, or only you, go off with a second tap. The description shows the real numbers.
  • A number on a button is its cooldown: how many of your turns it needs to be ready.
  • Guard: half damage until your next turn, 1 Ember, and your next turn comes sooner.
Statuses
  • Durations count the owner's turns. Poison, Bleed and Burn hit at the start of its turn. Tap any plate or icon to read it.
  • Stun skips a turn (bosses are Staggered instead). Silence leaves only the basic attack. Taunt pulls single-target attacks. Shields absorb damage first.
Between battles
  • Everyone is healed after a fight, and a defeat costs nothing: retry, or change something at the Camp.
  • At the Camp: gear (compare before you equip), the abilities you bring (3, then 4 from level 4), and the skill tree (one point per level, reset for free). Wren can swear only one Oath.
  • Take three of the four into each battle. Cleared battles can be replayed for half XP.
Controls
  • Mouse or touch for everything. Keyboard: 1 to 9 abilities, ← → or Tab targets, Enter confirm, Esc cancel, P pause.
  • Pause and Back to the title sit in the top-right cluster, with sound, fullscreen and exit.
